Showcase

NITRN (NITRN Is This Renderer's Name) - Unreleased

3D raymarcher I made around June 2024. Uses C++, OpenGL, GLFW, and Dear ImGui.

Features:

  • SDF raymarching & primitive raytracing
  • 3D anaglyph & depth of field
  • Save/load
  • Rendering to files
  • Monte Carlo path tracing
